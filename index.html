Intro

High school engineer and researcher

I'm a mechanical engineer at heart who learns by building. I work across the full design cycle: modeling parts in CAD, fabricating with CNC plasma, MIG/TIG welding, and 3D printing, and wiring electronics. I think in systems: I spot inefficiencies, prototype fixes, and test until they hold up. Then I teach others, so the work outlasts me.

Right now I'm working on 3D printing material research and special projects at Swagelok.
