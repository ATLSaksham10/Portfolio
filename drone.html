Part 107

Flight log

Part 107 is the FAA rule set for commercial small unmanned aircraft systems (small UAS) in the United States. It covers remote-pilot certification, aircraft registration, and operating limits for flights conducted as civil operations rather than hobby flying.

LAANC (Low Altitude Authorization and Notification Capability) is the FAA system that issues near-instant airspace authorizations for Part 107 flights in controlled airspace, through approved UAS Service Suppliers, instead of waiting on a manual waiver process.
