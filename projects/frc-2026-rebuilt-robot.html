Overview

As Mechanical Captain for the 2026 FRC season, REBUILT, I owned the robot's bumpers, integrated the intake and indexer subsystems, and worked on strengthening the superstructure. The robot is a fixed dual shooter with auto-aim and path systems, built on a CAN-based drivetrain.

Problem

REBUILT asks a robot to do four things well:

Drive

The field's central bumps and trenches call for a high-clearance drivetrain, typically swerve with articulating suspension, to keep speed while crossing them.

Intake

The compressible yellow foam Fuel needs a high-throughput roller intake using compliant wheels or polycord.

Score

Hitting the hexagonal Hub from multiple field positions needs a shooter that can adjust exit velocity, such as a variable-hood flywheel or an automated turret.

Climb

The endgame needs a heavy-duty telescoping hook or elevator lift that can rapidly raise the robot's full weight up the laddered central tower.

Approach

Bumpers

I read all of the bumper regulations and built the bumpers. I prototyped and developed custom brackets that mount the bumpers to the robot through the corner swerve bolts. They optimize the robot's perimeter and save upwards of 3 minutes between match rounds. One bracket is a CNC plasma-cut part that I welded to an L-bracket.

Intake and indexer

I integrated the intake and indexer subsystems, optimizing the gaps between them to increase ball throughput.

Superstructure

I used my mechanical systems knowledge to develop and strengthen the robot's superstructure.
