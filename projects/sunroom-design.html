Sunroom design

A code-compliant sunroom, from building-code research to an Onshape model and county-approved scaled drawings.

Role

Sole designer

Timeframe

Mar – Jul 2024 (freshman year)

Tools

Onshape, Forsyth County building codes

Team

Solo

Overview

In my freshman year I designed a sunroom from concept to permit-ready drawings. The design had to meet county, state, and community building codes, and my drawings were what got the project approved. Then I helped build it.

Problem

Design a sunroom that adheres to county, state, and community building codes and standards. The concept had to maximize sunlight and utility in the room without sacrificing strength, and ship as official scaled drawings and a 3D model.

Approach

Codes first

I researched the rules on roof angle to determine how far out the sunroom could extend, the percentage of wall that could be window, and the largest window sizes allowed without needing tempered glass.

CAD

With those limits set, I built the CAD model in Onshape, adjusting window and door placement until it met the needs of the home.

Results

Approved
County certification
Built
Sunroom completed

My drawings were used by the county for certification and approval, since they demonstrated adherence to all technical codes. The project went through, and I helped build the sunroom.

Key takeaways

  • CAD in industrial and residential applications
  • Designing around real-world constraints
