About

I'm an aspiring mechanical engineer who learns best by building, and I approach problems as systems to understand and improve. I'm comfortable across the full design cycle, from modeling parts in CAD to fabricating them with CNC plasma, MIG/TIG welding, and 3D printing, and integrating electronics.

At Caterpillar, I applied that mindset to manufacturing: I used bend-radius calculations and materials analysis to reduce wire friction in a crimping process, which increased machine feed capacity by 140% and extended feed lifespan by about 84%. At Swagelok, I expanded tubing inspection from 50% to 100% of tube types and helped restructure a warehouse workflow to eliminate operator backtracking. Whether I'm in a shop or on a production floor, I look for friction, test solutions rigorously, and share what I learn so improvements last.

I'm a senior at Denmark High School in Alpharetta, GA. I got into engineering through the Home Depot kids' kits. That's where I learned to use a hammer, and I haven't stopped since.
